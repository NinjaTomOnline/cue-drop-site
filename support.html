Cue Drop Support

Set up a cue

  1. Choose a song from your Apple Music library or import an unprotected M4A, MP3, or WAV file. Apple Music playback depends on access to your selected track.
  2. Open Edit, set the starting time, and use Preview 5 seconds to check it.
  3. Enter a short phrase. If you are using an external speaker, choose it in Control Center and test it before the performance.
  4. Tap Arm voice cue and allow microphone and speech recognition access. Keep Cue Drop in the foreground while it listens.

If the cue misses

Try a shorter phrase, bring the iPhone closer, or tap Play now. Speech recognition can time out or be unavailable on some devices. Use Stop & reset to cancel playback and reset the cue.

Contact

Email support@ninjatomapps.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue.
