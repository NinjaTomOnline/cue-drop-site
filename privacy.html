Cue Drop Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Cue Drop has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. Cue Drop does not send your recordings, cue settings, or speech to NinjaTom Apps.

Microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Cue Drop listens for a phrase while a voice cue is armed. It uses Apple’s on-device English speech recognition. Captured microphone audio is not saved to disk. The latest recognized text is shown while listening. Listening ends on a match, timeout, disarm, or when you leave the app.

Music and app settings

Cue Drop keeps your cue phrase and playback settings on the device. It stores a local copy of your chosen imported file or the identifier and display name of your selected Music library item. You can replace the file by choosing another audio source or remove app data by deleting Cue Drop.

Apple Music access and TestFlight diagnostics are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@ninjatomapps.com.
