Cue Drop

Start a recording on cue during a live performance. Choose a song from your Apple Music library or import an audio file, set a starting point, and arm a short spoken or sung phrase. Use Play now to start at the same point by hand.

Cue Drop runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Voice cues require on-device English speech recognition. The included demo chime lets you try the controls before choosing a recording.
